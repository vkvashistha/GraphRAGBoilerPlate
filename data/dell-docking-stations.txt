## COMPATIBILITY NOTICE FOR DELL PRO MAX 14 PREMIUM (MA14250)

The Dell Pro Max 14 Premium (MA14250) features the following ports:
- Two USB Type-C Thunderbolt 5 ports (up to 120 Gbps) with Power Delivery and DisplayPort 2.1
- Two USB Type-C Thunderbolt 4 ports (40 Gbps) with Power Delivery and DisplayPort 2.1
- One global headset port
- One microSD-card slot
- One USB Type-C power-adapter port
- One wedge-shaped lock slot

Please refer to the compatibility table below for each docking station model.

## DELL THUNDERBOLT DOCK WD22TB4
### Model: WD22TB4 | Regulatory Model: K20A | Regulatory Type: K20A001
### Compatibility: COMPATIBLE with Dell Pro Max 14 Premium (MA14250) - Thunderbolt 4 ports only

The Dell Thunderbolt Dock WD22TB4 is a premium docking solution designed for Dell laptops and mobile workstations equipped with Thunderbolt 4, Thunderbolt 3, USB-C, or USB4 ports. This dock delivers exceptional connectivity and power delivery in a compact form factor.

#### TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:

**Power Delivery:**
- Up to 130W power delivery to compatible Dell systems
- 90W standard power delivery to non-Dell systems via USB-PD
- 5V/3A (15W) charging for mobile devices on USB-A ports

**Video Outputs:**
- 2x DisplayPort 1.4
- 1x HDMI 2.0
- 1x Thunderbolt 4 downstream port (supports DisplayPort)
- Maximum resolution support:
  * Single display: 8K @ 60Hz
  * Dual displays: 4K @ 60Hz
  * Triple displays: 4K @ 30Hz
  * Quad displays: 1080p @ 60Hz

**Data Ports:**
- 1x Thunderbolt 4 upstream (host connection)
- 1x Thunderbolt 4 downstream (40Gbps)
- 3x USB-A 3.2 Gen 2 (10Gbps)
- 1x USB-A 3.2 Gen 1 with PowerShare (5Gbps)
- 1x USB-C 3.2 Gen 2 (10Gbps)

**Network Connectivity:**
- 2.5GbE RJ45 Ethernet port
- Optional Intel Wi-Fi 6E module (sold separately)

**Audio:**
- 1x 3.5mm Audio Combo Jack (headset)
- 1x 3.5mm Audio Line-Out

**Security Features:**
- Kensington lock slot
- Noble Wedge lock slot
- MAC address pass-through
- Dell Security Manager compatibility
- Wake-on-LAN support

**Dimensions:**
- Width: 205mm (8.07")
- Depth: 90mm (3.54")
- Height: 29mm (1.14")
- Weight: 585g (1.29 lbs)

**System Requirements:**
- Compatible with Dell laptops featuring Thunderbolt 4, Thunderbolt 3, or USB-C with DisplayPort Alt Mode
- Windows 10 (20H2 or later) or Windows 11
- macOS 12.0 or later (limited functionality)

**Warranty:**
- 3-year Advanced Exchange Service

**CAUTION:** Use only the power adapter provided with the dock. Using incompatible power adapters may damage the dock or connected devices.
